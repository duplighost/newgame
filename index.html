Quiet Hours

Wander an endless city at golden hour. Open doors and windows, or crouch low and crawl through, to explore the homes inside. No two streets are the same.

  • W A S D — move
  • Mouse — look
  • E — open door / window
  • C / Ctrl — crouch & crawl
  • Shift — sprint
  • Space — jump

On a phone: left stick to move, drag to look, and tap RUN · CROUCH · JUMP — the glowing button opens whatever you're facing.
